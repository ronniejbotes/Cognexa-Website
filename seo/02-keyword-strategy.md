## 0. Honesty note on volume — read before quoting any number below

**No paid keyword tool was used.** Every volume figure in this document is an
**estimate**, triangulated from Google Autocomplete depth with `gl=za`, back-solving
from Cognexa's own GSC data, and competitor page counts as a demand proxy.

Anchor point: `ai receptionist south africa` produced 8 impressions in 90 days at
position ~14, implying roughly **20–50 searches/month** — and that is one of the
*largest* purely commercial terms in this niche. Anyone quoting 1,000+/mo for an
SA-modified term here is quoting a global or padded figure.

> ### ⚠️ Do this before building anything from this table
>
> **Google Keyword Planner is free with any Google Ads account, even an unfunded one,
> and returns real South-Africa-geo volume ranges. Bing Webmaster Tools' Keyword
> Research is also free.** One hour of work replaces ~60 estimates below with actual
> numbers. This is the single cheapest way to de-risk the entire plan.

**The blunt headline:** almost every SA-specific term below sits between **10 and 300
searches/month**. That is not a reason to abandon the strategy. At R30,000–R200,000
deal sizes, nine qualified clicks a month is a viable business. The target is **intent
density across 60+ small terms**, not volume on any single head term.

## 1. The positioning decision

### The problem, in Google's own words

Six of your eleven GSC queries are Google flailing:

| Query | Impressions | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| `business` | 1 | loose token match |
| `business software` | 2 | loose token match |
| `businesses near me` | 1 | Google guessing at local intent |
| `businesses around me` | 1 | Google guessing at local intent |
| `local businesses without websites` | 1 | Google guessing |
| `botlhale ai` | 12 | **serving you for a competitor's brand** — 14% of your total visibility, converting nothing |

This is the fingerprint of a single-page site that claims four topics and owns none.

### The decision

> **Cognexa is the South African agency that automates the back office of an SME: the
> phone, the WhatsApp line, and the quote → booking → invoice → CRM spine behind them.**
>
> - **Wedge (entry point):** the AI receptionist
> - **Depth (revenue):** quoting, invoicing, bookings, email triage, custom CRMs
> - **Geography:** South Africa first, Gauteng anchored. Worldwide as long-tail only

### Why — the SERP evidence

- `ai automation agency` with `gl=za` returns **`ai automation agency south africa` at
  #2 globally**, ahead of India and the USA. Disproportionate SA interest in *hiring*
  an automation agency.
- The SA competitor set (DDM Tech, AgentK, aiautomatedsolutions, Ruppell) is a fight
  among equals. Nobody has meaningful authority. Winner is whoever builds real depth first.
- `ai receptionist` with `gl=za` returns `ai receptionist south africa` as the **#2
  suggestion**, ahead of Australia and the UK — and it is the only commercial query
  already producing impressions for you.
- The `custom crm` SERP has **three of six top results held by Indian outsourcing firms
  running templated `/south-africa/` pages** with no local proof, no ZAR pricing, no SA
  case studies.
- `email sorting AI south africa` returns **literally zero SA results**.

### What to de-emphasise

| Topic | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Immersive 3D websites** | **Demote hard.** One service page, shipped last, framed in automation language. Never blog about Three.js | `3d website design` autocomplete is *prompt, templates, free, ai, examples, ideas, portfolio, inspiration* — **100% DIY intent, 0% buyer intent.** It wins deals once someone is on the site; it is not a traffic channel, and as a co-equal fourth pillar it dilutes your topic focus |
| **Multilingual NLP as positioning** | **Reposition, don't abandon** | Botlhale AI is funded, Google-featured, and serves MTN/DSTV/H&M/Mukuru — all confirmed. Don't fight them at the enterprise end. **Nobody owns the SME end**: "an AI receptionist that speaks Afrikaans, for a small business" is genuinely unoccupied |
| **Generic `chatbot` head terms** | **Kill** | 6 of 10 autocomplete suggestions are DStv, MTN, Clearscore, Payflex, Betway support-bot lookups. Navigational, not commercial |
| **Global unqualified head terms** | **Kill for 12 months** | `ai receptionist cost` is wall-to-wall funded US SaaS in USD. `ai voice agent` autocomplete is *asterisk, github, builder* — developers, not buyers |

## 7. The under-served offerings problem

### The gap, measured — grep of `index.html`

| Term | Occurrences |
|---|---|
| `quoting` | **0** |
| `booking` / `bookings` | **0** |
| `email sort` | **0** |
| `custom CRM` | **0** |
| `invoic` | 4 *(passing)* |
| `CRM` | 4 |
| `receptionist` | 15 |

The founder's own description of the business — *"quoting, invoicing, bookings, email
sorting, CRM management, Custom CRMs"* — has effectively **zero representation on the
site**. Meanwhile these sit in the emptiest SERPs in the entire market.

**That inversion — four pillars aimed at the three most contested terms, while four
core offerings sit in near-empty SERPs and appear nowhere — is the single
highest-value thing to correct.**

### The fix

| Offering | Page | SERP state |
|---|---|---|
| **Quoting** | `automated-quoting-invoicing.html` | Only SaaS directories (Capterra, Inv24, TenderProSA). **Zero agencies compete** |
| **Invoicing** | same page | Only accounting firms and software vendors. **Zero automation agencies** |
| **Bookings** | `booking-automation-south-africa.html` | Booking SaaS competes; **nobody offers custom integrated builds**. Strongest confirmed SA volume of the four |
| **Email sorting** | *section inside* `workflow-automation-south-africa.html` | `email sorting AI south africa` returns **literally zero SA results**. But also near-zero demand — build it to close deals, not to earn traffic |
| **CRM management** | `custom-crm-development-south-africa.html` (automation §) | Bridges Clusters B and E |
| **Custom CRMs** | `custom-crm-development-south-africa.html` | **3 of 6 top results are Indian outsourcing templates.** Least contested, highest ticket in the map |

### Also fix the homepage itself

- `<title>` → `Cognexa | AI Automation Agency in South Africa`. **The current title does
  not contain the string "Cognexa"**, and `cognexa` is the #1 query at 15 impressions
  and 0 clicks.
- `<h1>` → `AI business automation for South African companies`, with *"Your business,
  on autopilot."* demoted to a subhead so the brand voice survives.
- The `#services` block's only H2 is `<h2 class="sr-only">Services</h2>` — **visually
  hidden**. Section H2s read "How the line runs", "Built on the line", "What the line
  delivers" — evocative and semantically worthless for retrieval.
- Rewrite the workflow section to **name the jobs explicitly**: automated quoting,
  automated invoicing, appointment booking and reminders, inbox triage, CRM data
  hygiene, custom CRM builds. Those are the strings buyers type.
- Add a **fifth service block for custom CRMs**; demote 3D websites below the three
  automation pillars.
- **Fix the four codename portfolio items** ("Nexus Core", "Nightgrid", "Gridworks",
  "Orbital" + *"full walkthroughs are private"*, `index.html:360–412`). Your only proof
  asset currently reads as a trust liability. This is a **10-minute copy edit — do it
  in week 1**, not week 13.
- Fix the broken sentence at `index.html:458`: *"there's you are never locked in"*.
